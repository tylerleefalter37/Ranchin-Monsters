📖 HOW TO PLAY: DISCSTONE 64 — MONSTER RANCHER & CODE64 SHRINE

Welcome to Discstone 64! Raise legendary creatures, decode secret CD phrases or shareable Code64 DNA strings at the Shrine, fuse hybrids in the Combining Lab, and battle to Class S in the IMA Arena.

1. 💿 Code64 Shrine & Trade Codes

In the Code64 Shrine tab, type ANY word, album, or phrase to deterministically summon a monster! Furthermore, every monster has a unique MR64-... DNA code—copy it from your Barn and paste it into the Shrine to trade or clone exact monsters!

2. 🏠 Unified Barn Roster

Just like the Mews in the Falconry game, all your monsters are stored in the Barn Roster tab with live animated canvas sprites. Click any monster to make it Active, export its Code64 string, or keep it safe for Fusion.

3. 🧬 Monster Combining (Fusion)

In the Fusion Lab, select Parent A (Main Body) and Parent B (Color/Aura/Markings) plus an optional Secret Relic Catalyst. High parent stats grant huge bonus starting stats and transfer learned techniques to the newborn!

4. 🏋️ Weekly Training & Errantry

Train your active monster in 6 core stats (LIF, POW, INT, SKI, SPD, DEF). Watch your Fatigue meter—use Rest Wk or feed Cup Jelly / Nuts Oil so your monster stays healthy. Send it on Errantry to learn powerful new Range Techniques!

5. 🏟️ Real-Time Guts Arena Combat

In the IMA Arena, shift between Near, Mid, and Far Range. Your Guts regenerate in real time; higher Guts boost your Hit/Dodge chance and let you unleash devastating signature Techniques!

6. 🛒 Golden Peaches & ⚡ God Mode

Visit the Shop for Golden Peaches (youth restoration), Stat Vitamins, and Ranch Upgrades. Want pure sandbox fun? Toggle ⚡ GOD: ON in the top bar for free shopping, +200 All Stats, and S-Rank promotion!
